Automated LinkedIn Content Pipeline

            
Daily research, writing, QA, and publishing workflow.

            
n8nAPI IntegrationLLM WorkflowsNo-Code/Low-Code

            
Problem: producing consistent, high-quality LinkedIn content manually takes hours each week. Solution: a daily n8n workflow loads configuration, researches topics through RSS and live web search, merges and dedupes results against content history, scores candidates, generates a draft with an LLM, and runs a separate humanize and quality-scoring pass.
